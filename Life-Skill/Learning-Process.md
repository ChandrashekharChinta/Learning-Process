Question 4
## - According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.
1. Deconstruct the skill - Break the skill down into its most basic parts. Which parts are necessary for hitting the goal.
2. Learn enough to self correct - Learn enough to realize when you're making mistakes.
3. Remove barriers to practice - Turn off your phone, unplug the TV. Put your guitar, piano, language book in the middle of your room.
4. Practice for at least 20 hours - Commit to 20 hours from the start and practice the skill over and over again.
