# Focus Management

### Question 1


**What is Deep Work?**

Deep work means working with complete focus and without distractions so that you can achieve your best performance.

### Question 2


**According to the author, how can you do deep work properly?**

* Stay completely focused for around an hour or 90 minutes.
* Avoid using social media while doing work.
* Do not switch between different tasks while working.

### Question 3


**How can you implement the principles of deep work in your day-to-day life?**

* Set clear goals and deadlines for the work you need to complete first.
* Set aside specific periods of time for distraction.
* Practice focused and disciplined work and time.
* Avoid multitasking and concentrate on one task at a single time.

### Question 4


**What are the dangers of social media, in brief?**

* It wastes a lot of productive time.
* It exposes people to negative or hateful content.
* It affects  sleep and lead to sleep deprivation.
* It spreads  misinformation.
* Excessive use can negatively affect mental well-being and may contribute to depression.
