## Q1. What activities make you feel calm – Calm Quadrant?

1. I sit quietly for some time.
2. I pray in my own simple way.
3. I listen to positive talks.
4. I read small story books that give good thoughts.
5. I go to peaceful places when I feel low.
6. I listen to songs I like.
7. I do small drawings or simple art.
8. I spend time outside and look at trees and sky.
9. I sing my favorite songs.
10. I eat my favorite food happily.
11. I help someone when they need support.
12. I do some useful work to keep myself busy.
13. I watch funny videos.
14. I sit and talk with my mother.

---

## Q2. When do you enter the Stress Quadrant?

I feel stressed when I compare myself with other people.  
I think they are better than me and I feel sad.

---

## Q3. How do you know you are in the Excitement Quadrant?

I feel very happy and active.  
My mind feels free.  
I feel thankful for what I have.

---

## Q4. Sleep Is Your Superpower (Paraphrased Points)

- Sleeping well keeps the body healthy.
- Less sleep can affect our health.
- Small naps help us learn better.
- Sleeping after studying helps us remember.
- One bad night can make us forget things.
- Deep sleep helps keep memories for a long time.
- Less sleep makes our body weak.
- Not sleeping properly can cause health problems.

---

## Q5. What can you do to sleep better?

1. Sleep at the same time every day.
2. Stop using the phone before bed.
3. Take deep breaths if feeling stressed.
4. Keep the room quiet and dark.

---

## Q6. Brain-Changing Benefits of Exercise (Paraphrased Points)

- Exercise makes us feel happy.
- It reduces stress.
- It helps us remember better.
- It improves focus.
- It keeps the brain strong.

---

## Q7. Steps to Exercise More

1. Walk every day.
2. Do small exercises at home.
3. Use stairs instead of lift.
4. Play outdoor games.
